These terms explain how orders placed through The Good Earth shop work. Please review them before ordering.

Placing an order

When you submit an order, you are asking us to provide the listed chicken products for delivery. We will contact you by phone to confirm availability, delivery details, and the delivery fee. An order is confirmed when we have contacted you and agreed those details.

Prices and payment

Prices are shown in Kenyan shillings (KSh). The delivery fee is not included in the displayed subtotal and will be confirmed by phone. This website does not take online payment; payment is due when the order is delivered, as confirmed with you.

Product details and availability

Product descriptions and weights are estimates for guidance. We will contact you if an item is unavailable or if there is a material change to your order before delivery.

Delivery and changes

Delivery timing and location are confirmed by phone. Contact us as soon as possible if you need to change or cancel an order. We will explain whether a change is still possible based on the order’s preparation and delivery status.

Food handling

Chicken is a perishable food. Refrigerate it promptly after delivery and handle and cook it safely before eating. Contact us promptly if there is a problem with an item you receive so we can discuss it with you.

Accounts

You may sign in with Google to associate orders with your shop account. Keep access to your Google account secure and provide accurate contact and delivery information when ordering.

Contact

For order questions, changes, or concerns, contact us at infinitesyrup.xyz@gmail.com.
